Outbreaks of paralytic shellfish poisoning, or PSP, are specifically caused by neurotoxins produced by a toxic, single-celled species of marine algae known as Alexandrium catinella.
Shellfish like clams, mussels, and crabs eat microscopic algae, including Alexandrium.
During a bloom of Alexandrium, the concentrations of neurotoxins can reach dangerous levels in the tissues of these shellfish,
making them unsafe to eat.
