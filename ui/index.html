      Privacy

      pktito turns speech into text on this computer. Your recordings, transcripts and settings stay here. pktito doesn't send them anywhere, and it has no accounts, ads or tracking.

      What reaches the internet

      Downloading a model. pktito downloads its speech model from models.pktito.net, which bunny.net, a content delivery network, serves. As with any website, the server receives your IP address, the time, the file requested and a standard user-agent string. pktito sends nothing else: no name, account or device ID, and nothing from your files. The server logs these requests to keep the service secure and working. We use the logs only to count downloads in aggregate. We don't use them to identify you, combine them with other data, or sell or share them.

      Links. Links in pktito open in your browser, and each site's own privacy policy applies there.

      Your choices

      To skip the download, use a model file you already have: choose “Open a model file…”. Clearing the recent list deletes its transcripts. Uninstalling pktito removes the app. Its data folder, with your settings, models and recent transcripts, stays until you delete it.
